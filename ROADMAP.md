# BarberAI Project Roadmap

## Phase 1: The "Wedge" – AI-Native Barbershop Scheduler (MVP)

**Goal:** A WhatsApp agent capable of managing a barber's daily appointments natively in conversation, hooked up to a simple companion mobile/web application for the barber to track the AI's actions.

## Phase 2: Upselling & Service Customization

**Goal:** The agent learns to handle hair-specific workflows. It can upsell services (e.g., "Would you like a beard trim with that haircut?"), handle different slot durations per service type, and answer barbershop-specific FAQs (e.g., "Do you do chemical hair straightening?").

## Phase 3: Multi-Chair / Staff Management

**Goal:** Moving from a single barber (Solopreneur) to a full barbershop shop floor. The agent can route bookings based on staff availability and customer preference ("Book me with Eli, or whoever is free first").

## Phase 4: The Full Business OS & Financial Integration

**Goal:** Automated post-haircut loops. Generating digital invoices via tax-authority-approved integrations (as discussed previously), managing a simplified inventory of hair products, and sending automated re-engagement texts (e.g., "It's been 3 weeks since your last fade, want to book your usual spot?").
